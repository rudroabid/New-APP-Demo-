Progression — How To Actually Get Bigger

We use double progression. This is non-negotiable — it's the engine of the whole plan.

1
Pick a starting weight

Choose a load you can lift for the bottom of the rep range with 1–2 reps still in reserve.

2
Add reps weekly

Each session, try to add at least one rep to each set versus last week. Log every set: weight × reps.

3
Hit the top → add weight

Once you reach the top of the rep range on all sets, add the smallest jump (2.5 kg / next dumbbell / one pin) and start again at the bottom.

4
Deload every 8–10 weeks

One easy week — halve your sets, keep the weight light. Lets joints and your nervous system recover so you come back stronger.

BEFORE YOU START

This plan is general fitness coaching, not medical advice. If you have any injuries, health conditions, or it's been a while since exercise, get cleared by a doctor first. Any supplement — check it's halal-certified and run it past a pharmacist if unsure.
